# Database Project Proposal

Project Name: BookVerse - Digital Library and Online Bookstore

Driven by my passion for reading, particularly comics, and my desire for a platform that combines reading with the ability to discover related publications and merchandise without switching between multiple websites, I decided to create BookVerse.

  ## 1. Application Overview

BookVerse is a platform that combines a digital library with an online bookstore. Its purpose is to provide a space where users can search for books, manga, manhwa, and light novels, create personal reading lists, and read content directly online.

BookVerse offers two ways to access books: through a membership subscription or by purchasing individual titles. Active members gain access to the majority of the library's collection, while certain premium or exclusive content may require a separate purchase.

Beyond digital reading, the application includes an online store where users can purchase physical copies, special editions, and related merchandise. Additionally, it provides updates on upcoming events, such as book signings, pop-up shops, and new product releases.

Ultimately, BookVerse aims to be an all-in-one platform where readers can enjoy books, manage their personal libraries, shop for related items, and stay informed about book-related events.

  ## 2. Key Features

 - Search and Discovery

Users can browse and search for books by title, author, genre, or category. The library includes a diverse range of reading materials, including novels, manga, manhwa.

 - Personal Library

Users can create their own reading lists, organize their collections, and track their reading progress. Books will remain in a user's library even if their subscription expires.

 - Access and Membership

BookVerse offers a subscription-based membership plan that grants users access to most books in the digital library, excluding certain premium or exclusive titles.

If a user's subscription expires, access to books included in the subscription plan will be restricted. However, the user's personal reading lists and reading progress will remain saved.

 - Individual Purchases

Users also have the option to purchase individual digital titles. Unlike subscription-based access, books purchased individually remain accessible even after a user's subscription expires.

Certain premium or exclusive titles may only be available through individual purchases.

 - Online Store and Merchandise

The application includes an online store offering physical books, special editions, and merchandise such as photocards, acrylic stands, postcards, and keychains.

 - Events and Announcements

Users can view information about upcoming events, including book signings, anime conventions, special releases, and promotional activities.

  ## 3. Planned Database Entities

The database will include the following core entities:

1. *Users:* Stores information about registered users and their accounts.

2. *Books:* Stores information about books.

3. *Authors:* Stores information about authors and creators associated with books.

4. *Reading Lists:* Stores books saved by users for future reading.

5. *Reading Progress:* Tracks users' reading progress.

6. *Membership Plans:* Stores information about available membership plans.

7. *Subscriptions:* Tracks user memberships, including subscription status, start dates, and expiration dates.

8. *Book Purchases:* Records digital books purchased individually by users.

9. *Products:* Stores information about physical books, special editions, and merchandise available in the online store.

10. *Orders:* Stores information about customer purchases and order status.

11. *Events:* Stores information about upcoming events, including event names, dates, locations, and descriptions.

 ## 4. Project Objectives

- Create a database that supports both digital library and bookstore functionalities.
- Enable users to search for books and manage their personal reading lists.
- Support different reading access rules based on membership status and individual book purchases.
- Preserve saved books and reading progress even after a membership expires.
- Support product searches, merchandise management, customer orders, and purchase records.
- Provide information about upcoming events.

 ## 5. Project Scope

The initial development phase will focus primarily on book management, personal reading lists, membership subscriptions, and individual e-book purchases.

Additional tables and features, particularly those related to merchandise sales and events, may be developed in later stages.
